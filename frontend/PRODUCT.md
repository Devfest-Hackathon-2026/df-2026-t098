## Platform

adaptive

WhyLint uses Angular on the web and is intended to run in iOS and Android applications through Capacitor. Adapt the experience to the mobile OS where appropriate.

## Users and purpose

Students and early-career developers use WhyLint to understand AI-assisted code. The core experience is a phone-first, vertically scrolling practice feed that asks learners to explain why code works.

## Constraints

- The team is building WhyLint for the GDGoC Japan DevFest 2026 hackathon.
- The app uses Angular, Capacitor, NestJS, PostgreSQL and TypeORM.
- Japanese is the default interface language; English is also supported.
- This checkout extends the existing visual implementation and must preserve its design and behavior.
- The existing feed is a preview; do not present mock behavior as a completed backend integration.

## Principles

- Put a learner's own explanation at the center of practice.
- Make the feed immediate and comfortable on a phone, with desktop as a fallback.
- Be clear about what has and has not been connected or verified.
